❓ How to Play

  1. Pick a seed packet at the bottom, then tap a square to plant it. Slide your finger before you lift if you aimed badly — nothing happens until you let go.
  2. Everything costs 💧 water. Wellsprings make more of it. Plant those first, every single time.
  3. Tap the water drops to collect them — or leave them, they come to you in the end.
  4. Pests walk down the beds toward your gate. Each row has a 🧑‍🌾 scarecrow that saves it once. After that, one pest through the gate and the level is over.
  5. Not everything can be beaten the same way. A 🐚 shell shrugs off ordinary shots, and anything with wings flies straight over the ground. Check the 📖 Almanac.
  6. Press 📣 to call the next wave in early for bonus water, and ⏩ to speed the whole thing up.
